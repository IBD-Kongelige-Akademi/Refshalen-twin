Aalborg Twin – credits

A playable model of central Aalborg built from open Danish geodata, sponsored by Ramboll. ← Back to the city

Software

  • Slop New York by mrdoob (MIT): renderer, post-processing, controls, cameras, audio, traffic and pedestrians.
  • copenhagen-twin by Martin Collignon (MIT): the geodata pipeline and streaming city engine this project is forked from.
  • three.js (MIT): WebGPU renderer and TSL shaders.

Map and building data

Indeholder data fra Klimadatastyrelsen / Styrelsen for Dataforsyning og Infrastruktur, under the terms for free geographic data (CC BY 4.0):

  • GeoDanmark: buildings, roads, lakes, coast, harbour, trees, masts, parking, woods and parks.
  • DAR (addresses), BBR (building register) and DAGI (administrative areas).
  • DHM, Danmarks Højdemodel: terrain, building heights and vegetation heights.
  • Skråfoto 2021 (oblique aerial photos): building facade textures and roof colours.

Vector data read through datagrundlag.dk (MapCentia's mirror of the public data).

  • © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (ODbL): roads, traffic signals, lamps, water, parks, bridges, railways, nightlife venues and bus lines.
  • LoD2 roof shapes: CityJSON building model by Ramboll, made with Geoflow (MIT).

Landmark buildings

Six buildings are modelled by hand. Placement and footprints come from GeoDanmark Bygning, and colours and measurements from Skråfoto 2021 (both above). The drawings named below are from Aalborg Kommune's building archive (byggesagsarkiv) on weblager.dk; only measurements taken from them are in the models.

Aalborg Kongres & Kultur Center

  • Roof levels, eaves and ridges from the DHM point cloud.
  • Reference photos (not used as textures): Leonhard Lenz, Wikimedia Commons, CC0; Drlady, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0; Liberaler Humanist, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0.

Musikkens Hus

  • Roof levels from the DHM point cloud and surface model.
  • Reference photo (not used as a texture): Marc Søgaard, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0.

Utzon Center

  • Roof levels from the Skråfoto surface model.
  • Reference photos for the brick infill and lanterns (not used as textures): Liberaler Humanist, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0.

Budolfi Domkirke

  • Drawings from the building archive: Sankt Budolfi – Aalborg Domkirke, hovedrenovering 1. afsnit, Arkitekter Dall & Lindhardtsen A/S, 2000 (after the survey of 1986, Brøgger arkitektfirma), and Budolfi Kirke: opmaaling af spiret, 1934.

Ansgars Kirke

  • Drawings from the building archive: Kirke til Budolfi Sogns sydvestre del / Ny Sognekirke, Aalborg, facade drawings, 1925.

Hotel Det Hvide Hus

  • Drawings from the building archive: Hotel ved Kilden, Aalborg, K. Friis og E. Moltke Nielsen, arkitekter, 1967; Hotel Hvide Hus: udskiftning af facadepartier, Jørgen Braad A/S, 2005; opholdsterrasse mod Kildeparken, Peter Sørensen, landskabsarkitekt, 2002.
