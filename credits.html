Refshaleøen Twin – credits

A playable model of Refshaleøen, Holmen and Frederiksstaden in Copenhagen, built from open Danish geodata at Det Kongelige Akademi, on Andreas' Aalborg Twin. ← Back to the city

Software

  • Slop New York by mrdoob (MIT): renderer, post-processing, controls, cameras, audio, traffic and pedestrians.
  • copenhagen-twin by Martin Collignon (MIT): the geodata pipeline and streaming city engine this project is forked from.
  • three.js (MIT): WebGPU renderer and TSL shaders.

Map and building data

Indeholder data fra Klimadatastyrelsen / Styrelsen for Dataforsyning og Infrastruktur, under the terms for free geographic data (CC BY 4.0):

  • GeoDanmark: buildings, roads, lakes, coast, harbour, trees, masts, parking, woods and parks.
  • DAR (addresses), BBR (building register) and DAGI (administrative areas).
  • DHM, Danmarks Højdemodel: terrain, building heights and vegetation heights.
  • Skråfoto 2021 (oblique aerial photos): building facade textures and roof colours.

Vector data read through datagrundlag.dk (MapCentia's mirror of the public data).

  • © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (ODbL): roads, traffic signals, lamps, water, parks, bridges, railways, nightlife venues and bus lines.
  • LoD2 roof shapes: reconstructed from the DHM point cloud (scan 2019) with roofer (3DBAG, MIT/Apache).
  • Aalborg Twin by Andreas (askonring/aalborg-twin, MIT): the project this one is forked from.

Landmark buildings

Bygning 147, Refshalevej 147

  • Rhino model and facade photographs by Anders Hermund, Det Kongelige Akademi.

KAI, Vandkulturhuset Papirøen

  • Built from measurements read off the published plans and sections (Kengo Kuma & Associates, Cornelius Vöge, Søren Jensen; executing architect Vilhelm Lauritzen Arkitekter). The drawings themselves are not part of the model; windows and textures are indicative.

Refshaleøen Twin – kreditering

En spilbar model af Refshaleøen, Holmen og Frederiksstaden i København, bygget af åbne danske geodata på Det Kongelige Akademi, oven på Andreas' Aalborg Twin. ← Tilbage til byen

Software

  • Slop New York af mrdoob (MIT): renderer, efterbehandling, styring, kameraer, lyd, trafik og fodgængere.
  • copenhagen-twin af Martin Collignon (MIT): geodata-pipelinen og den streamende bymotor, som projektet er forgrenet fra.
  • three.js (MIT): WebGPU-renderer og TSL-shadere.

Kort- og bygningsdata

Indeholder data fra Klimadatastyrelsen / Styrelsen for Dataforsyning og Infrastruktur, under vilkår for frie geografiske data (CC BY 4.0):

  • GeoDanmark: bygninger, veje, søer, kyst, havn, træer, master, parkering, skove og parker.
  • DAR (adresser), BBR (Bygnings- og Boligregistret) og DAGI (administrative inddelinger).
  • DHM, Danmarks Højdemodel: terræn, bygningshøjder og vegetationshøjder.
  • Skråfoto 2021 (skrå luftfotos): facadeteksturer og tagfarver.

Vektordata hentet via datagrundlag.dk (MapCentias spejling af de offentlige data).

  • © OpenStreetMap-bidragydere, Open Database License (ODbL): veje, lyssignaler, lygter, vand, parker, broer, jernbaner, nattelivssteder og buslinjer.
  • LoD2-tagformer: rekonstrueret fra DHM-punktskyen (scanning 2019) med roofer (3DBAG, MIT/Apache).
  • Aalborg Twin af Andreas (askonring/aalborg-twin, MIT): projektet, som dette er forgrenet fra.
